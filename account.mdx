# Account & Profile

Access your account page via the sidebar. There you can:

- Edit your username and avatar.
- View your Credits balance and unified billing history.
- See purchase history and total images generated.
- Rate your experience (1-5 stars).
- Manage Pro and Add-on Credits from the Billing page.
