# Community Forum

Welcome to the [AtlasFlux AI community](https://ai.atlasflux.my/community) – a space where you can share your AI creations, discover what others are building, and interact with fellow creators. The community is currently in beta, and we are rapidly adding new features.

## Feed & Discovery

Your main entry point is the [Community Feed](https://ai.atlasflux.my/community). Every auto-shared AI generation appears in a visual grid. Sort by **Popular**, **Newest**, or **All Time Favourites**.

## Auto-Sharing Your Creations

Enable **Auto-Share** in [Preferences](https://ai.atlasflux.my/account/preferences). Each generation includes the prompt, model, settings, and reference images as clickable thumbnails.

## Recreate Button

Every community post features a **Recreate** button. Clicking it opens the corresponding AI tool (AI Image or Video Generation) with all settings, prompt, and reference images pre-filled. Anyone can recreate any post.

## Content Moderation (Take Down)

A team of moderators can take down posts that violate community guidelines. The post owner receives an email and a system notification. The post is blurred and marked as taken down, but the owner can still view it and submit an appeal.

## User Profile

Every user has a public profile showing their bio, country, links, followers, and creations. Your own profile includes a **Private** tab for taken-down or manually privatised posts.

## Explore Page & Bento Grid

The [Explore](https://ai.atlasflux.my/community/explore) page offers a dense, zero-gap bento grid with a powerful live search bar. Search for users or posts by name, and filter by content type.

## Saved Posts

Bookmark any post by clicking the **bookmark icon**. Access all saved posts at [/community/saved](https://ai.atlasflux.my/community/saved). Unsave directly from the grid.

## Following & Followers

Follow other creators to build your network. The follower/following count opens a modal listing all users, with one-click **Follow** / **Unfollow**.

## Notifications

All community interactions generate notifications (likes, comments, follows, takedowns) shown in the **bell icon**. System notifications use the official AtlasFlux AI avatar with a verified badge.

## Migrating Old Content

If you used the image/video tools before the community launched, a migration script can backfill your history into your public profile (respects your Auto-Share setting).
