# Credit System – How It Works

## Daily Credits

Free plan users receive **30 free credits** every day (Pro users receive **50**), resetting at midnight Malaysia Time (UTC+8). Unused daily credits do not roll over to the next day.

## Bonus Credits

Bonus Credits never expire. They come from:

- Welcome bonus: 120 credits on first sign-in.
- Purchased Add-on Credits.
- Special promotions.

Credits are consumed in this order: **daily credits first**, then bonus credits.

## Token-Based Pricing

Chat and Agent usage is charged **based on actual token usage**. Free models cost nothing; premium models are charged per token, and the rate depends on the selected model. The cost is displayed before you send your message.

## Automatic Refunds

If all models fail to respond, the full credit amount is refunded. Check your [Credit Logs](https://ai.atlasflux.my/account) for verification.
