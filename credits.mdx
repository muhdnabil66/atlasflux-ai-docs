# Credits – One Balance for Every AI Tool

Chat, Agent, AI Image, Video, and paid tools use Credits. **1 Credit equals RM0.01.** Pro unlocks premium model access, while every billable generation continues to consume Credits based on its displayed cost.

## How to Add Credits

Visit the [Billing page](https://ai.atlasflux.my/purchase). Enter any amount from RM10 to RM500 and continue to Stripe. Every sen paid becomes one Credit; for example, RM10 adds 1,000 Credits.

## Using Credits for Image and Video

Select a model and settings in the [AI Image](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools/ai-image) or [Video](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools/video-generation) tool. Credits are reserved before the provider runs, captured only after success, and released automatically after a failed or blocked generation.

## Billing History

Credit additions and usage are recorded in your billing logs. Purchase History combines Pro, Credits, add-on, renewal, refund, and future Stripe transactions with invoice or receipt access.

## Key Advantages

- One balance for chat, Agent, image, video, and tools.
- Transparent pricing – 100 Credits always equals RM1.
- Automatic release when a reserved generation fails.
- Secure payments processed by Stripe.
