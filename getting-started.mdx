# Getting Started

You can begin using AtlasFlux AI right after signing in with a free account – sign-in is required. Signed-in users get persistent history, tools, and community features.

## Creating an Account

Click the **Sign In** button located at the top right corner and choose your preferred sign-in method: email (passwordless OTP), Google, or GitHub. After signing in, you will instantly receive a one-time welcome bonus of 120 credits. Your account syncs seamlessly across all your devices, and all history is securely stored in the cloud. **Signed-in users can store up to 500 chat sessions.**

## Interface Overview

The left sidebar provides access to essential features: New Chat, the Tools Hub, Credits balance, billing, contact, legal policies, and account settings. The main area displays your active chat or the workspace of the selected tool. The top bar shows your Credit balance, language toggle, theme switcher, notifications, and profile.

## The Tools Hub (AtlasFlux Studio)

Click the **Tools icon (⚙️)** in the sidebar to open [AtlasFlux Studio](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools) – the central launchpad for all AI creation tools. Inside you will find: AI Image Generator, Video Generation, Music, Voice Cloning, Audio Separation, Paraphrase, Prompt Creator, Face Restoration, Remove Background, Upscaler, Image to 3D, and Image Tools. Each tool has its own dedicated page with custom settings and a comprehensive history.
