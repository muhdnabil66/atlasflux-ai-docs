# AI Image Generation (Credit-Based)

Access this tool from the [Tools Hub](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools) → [AI Image](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools/ai-image). More than 100 models support text-to-image, image-to-image, and multi-reference generation. Each model displays its unique settings and estimated Credit cost. Every successful generation is charged from your unified Credits balance.

> **Pricing note:** AI Image models use Credit-based pricing. The displayed Credit estimate varies by model and settings; Credits are captured only after a successful generation.

## Choosing a Model

Select a model from the dropdown menu. The settings panel adapts dynamically – showing only the options supported by that model, such as resolution, aspect ratio, safety tolerance, seed, style, and more. The estimated Credit cost updates in real-time as you adjust the settings.

## Image-to-Image & Reference Images

For models that support img2img, you can upload one or more reference images. The AI will follow your prompt and use the provided visuals for guidance. Some models support up to 14 reference images (e.g., Nano Banana Pro). If a model does not support image input, the upload area is automatically disabled.

## Advanced Settings

Depending on the model, you can tweak aspect ratio, resolution, safety tolerance, seed, style, negative prompt, and more. All changes update the Credit cost instantly, giving you full control over both the output quality and the cost.

## Image History

All generated images are saved to your [AI Image History](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools/ai-image/history). From there, you can preview, download, share, pin (to keep beyond 7 days), or delete any image. A retention banner reminds you of the 7-day auto-cleanup for unpinned items.

## Community Auto-Share

If you enable **Auto-Share** in your [Preferences](https://ai.atlasflux.my/account/preferences), every generated image is automatically posted to the [Community Feed](https://ai.atlasflux.my/community). Each post includes the prompt, model, settings, and any reference images used. You can disable this feature at any time from your preferences.
