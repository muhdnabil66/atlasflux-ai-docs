# Introduction to AtlasFlux AI

AtlasFlux AI is a next-generation platform that aggregates **over 400 cutting-edge AI models** from multiple leading AI providers. It unifies AI chat, file analysis, image generation, video creation, music composition, voice cloning, audio separation, 3D modeling, and a comprehensive suite of free text tools under one transparent Credits system. One Credit always represents RM0.01 of usage value. **AtlasFlux AI is operated by RAINSPEED LABS SOLUTIONS (Reg. No. 202603148143 / 003857582-W), a Malaysian-registered company.**

Free plan users receive 30 free Credits daily (Pro users receive 50), with optional Add-on Credits for additional usage. Chat, Agent, AI Image, Video, and paid tools all consume the same Credit balance. The platform automatically selects the most suitable model for each request, but also offers a Manual Mode for those who desire full control. Enhanced features include **Agent Mode** (autonomous task solving with code generation) and intelligent model selection with reasoning capabilities built into the models themselves.

AtlasFlux AI is designed for accessibility – installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA), and available in both English and Malay. **All users must sign in** to use AtlasFlux AI – an account is required. All user data is protected by Row Level Security, ensuring your privacy. The platform also features a vibrant community where you can share creations, follow other creators, and discover inspiring content. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://ai.atlasflux.my/legal/privacy).
