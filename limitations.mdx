# Known Limitations

- **Chat sessions**: max 500 per signed-in user (oldest unpinned auto-deleted). Guests: 0 sessions (no history).
- **Generated media**: auto-removed after 7 days unless pinned.
- **Video generation**: 2-5 min processing; model-specific limits on duration/resolution.
- **3D models**: large models may not preview; download recommended.
- **Manual Mode** disables Agent Mode; the selected model must support the attached files.
