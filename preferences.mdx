# Preferences

Customise your experience in the [Preferences](https://ai.atlasflux.my/account/preferences) tab.

- **Language**: toggle between **English** and **Bahasa Melayu**.
- **Appearance**: choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** theme.
- **Notifications**: toggle to enable or disable push notifications (bell icon).
- **Memory**: enable or disable automatic memory. When ON, the AI learns facts about you from conversations and uses them in future chats.
- **AI Voice**: select a text-to-speech voice from **43 options**.
- **Custom Instructions**: write a paragraph describing your preferences (e.g., 'I prefer concise answers'). This is sent as a system prompt to every chat.
- **Auto-Share to Community**: when enabled, your generated images and videos are automatically shared to the [community feed](https://ai.atlasflux.my/community).

## Skills Library

The [Skills Library](https://ai.atlasflux.my/account/skills) lets you browse and activate markdown skill files. Each skill contains instructions that the AI will follow when enabled. Skills are like pre-written system prompts for specific tasks (e.g., "Write Python code with explanations", "Answer in bullet points"). You can enable or disable them at any time from the skills panel.

## Rename & Pin Sessions

In the chat sidebar, click the **three dots** next to any session to rename it or mark it as starred (pinned). Starred sessions appear at the top of the list. Renamed titles are saved locally and synced to the cloud if you are signed in.

## Notifications System

The **bell icon** in the top bar shows all your notifications: community interactions (likes, comments, follows), system announcements, ticket updates, and takedown notices. Unread notifications are highlighted. Click a notification to view details; some notifications open a modal or redirect to relevant pages.
