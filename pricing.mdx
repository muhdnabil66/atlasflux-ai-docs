# Pricing & Credit Costs

Complete list of all AI models available on AtlasFlux, including credits cost and supported features. Prices are subject to change; check back for updates.

## Model Pricing

### AI Chat Models

Chat models are priced per token or per message depending on the model. Free models cost nothing; premium models use token-based pricing. You're charged **based on actual token usage**, and the exact cost is shown in the model selector.

### AI Image Models

Image models charge a fixed number of Credits per generated image, varying by model and selected settings (resolution, quality, aspect ratio).

### AI Video Models

Video models charge Credits per second or per configuration. The estimate depends on resolution, duration, audio, and the selected model.

### AI Music Models

Music models charge Credits per generation. Lyrics generation is free; audio generation costs 30-50 credits depending on the model.
