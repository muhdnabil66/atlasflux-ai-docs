# Pro & Add-on Credits

AtlasFlux has two payment options and one usage balance. Pro is a monthly subscription that unlocks premium model access. Add-on Credits are custom one-time payments from RM10 to RM500 and do not unlock Pro by themselves.

- **Free**: RM0 with curated model access and daily Credits.
- **Pro**: RM29.90/month for premium model access.
- **Add-on Credits**: custom RM10-RM500, where RM1 equals 100 Credits.

## Purchase Process

1. Open the [Billing page](https://ai.atlasflux.my/purchase).
2. Choose Pro or enter a custom Add-on Credit amount.
3. Complete payment through Stripe Checkout.
4. The Stripe webhook activates Pro or adds Credits idempotently.

> **Note:** All Credit purchases are non-refundable after completion.

## Robin 2 Pro (Pro-Only Flagship Agent)

Robin 2 Pro is the new flagship Agent model built exclusively for Pro users. It appears first in the Agent model picker and acts as the default auto-router for Pro users.

Robin 2 Pro usage is fully subsidised for Pro users with a daily allowance of 2,000,000 tokens (resetting on a 24-hour window in Malaysia time). When the daily limit is reached, the system automatically falls back to other available Agent models.

Check your Robin usage anytime under Account → Robin Usage.
