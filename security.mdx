# Security Settings

Manage your account security in the [Security](https://ai.atlasflux.my/account/security) tab.

- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Enable TOTP-based MFA for additional login protection.
- **Passkeys**: Add a passkey for biometric login (fingerprint, face).
- **Change Password**: Update your password by entering the current one and a new one (min 8 characters).
- **Active Sessions**: View and revoke devices where you are logged in. **Sign Out Everywhere** logs out all devices at once.
- **Email/Phone Verification**: Verify your email or add a phone number for recovery.
