# Help Center

For billing, account, technical, or general assistance, visit the [AtlasFlux Help Center](https://support.atlasflux.my).

The Help Center is the current support channel for AtlasFlux AI. It replaces the old contact form and in-app ticket dashboard.

## Before contacting support

Include the relevant account email, a clear description of the issue, and any useful details such as an order ID, model name, or approximate time of the problem. Do not share passwords, API keys, or other sensitive credentials.

## Account deletion

For account deletion assistance, use the [AtlasFlux Help Center](https://support.atlasflux.my).
