# Troubleshooting

- **Insufficient Credits** – wait for the daily reset, use bonus Credits, or purchase Add-on Credits from the [Billing page](https://ai.atlasflux.my/purchase).
- **Blank response** – credits refunded; refresh and retry.
- **Image not in history** – wait a few seconds or refresh the page.
- **Cannot sign in** – check internet/pop-up blockers; try a different browser.
- **Model not supporting image input** – the AI Image tool disables the upload area for incompatible models.
- **Video generation takes too long** – reduce duration or resolution. Try a faster model.
- **PWA not installing** – ensure you are on a secure connection (HTTPS).
- **Artifacts not saving** – ensure Agent Mode was activated when the code was generated.
