# Video Generation (Credit-Based)

Access this tool from the [Tools Hub](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools) → [Video Gen](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools/video-generation). Create videos from text, images, or existing clips. Models include Kling, Veo, Seedance, Hailuo, PixVerse, Wan, and many more. Each successful generation is charged in Credits from your unified balance.

> **Pricing note:** Video models charge Credits per second or configuration. The estimate depends on resolution, duration, audio, and the selected model.

## Choosing a Model

The dropdown lists all available video models. The form only shows fields supported by the selected model, such as resolution, duration, aspect ratio, audio, seed, fps, motion strength, expression scale, and more. The Credit cost updates live as you adjust settings.

## Settings & Cost

Configure resolution, duration, aspect ratio, audio generation, negative prompt, CFG scale, and more. The estimated Credit cost updates in real-time. A confirmation modal appears before generation with a request summary and estimated Credit charge.

## Input Types

Depending on the model, you can provide:

- Text prompt only (T2V)
- Reference image(s) for image-to-video
- Input video for extension/editing
- Audio file for lip-sync or audio-driven animation
- Reference images for style/character consistency

## Video History

Generated videos are saved to your [Video History](https://ai.atlasflux.my/tools/video-generation/history). You can play, download, share, pin (kept indefinitely), or delete any video. A retention notice reminds you of the 7-day auto-cleanup for unpinned items.
